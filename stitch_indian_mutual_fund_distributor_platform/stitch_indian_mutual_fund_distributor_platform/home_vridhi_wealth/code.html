Investor Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding regulatory status, money safety, and execution processes.

Is Vridhi Wealth an AMFI-registered distributor? verified

Yes. Vridhi Wealth is a registered Mutual Fund Distributor with the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) under registration number ARN-184920 and EUIN E392810. We operate strictly under SEBI mutual fund guidelines.

Are my funds safe? Where does my invested money go? security

Your money never touches Vridhi Wealth's accounts. All financial transactions are directly routed through BSE StAR MF (India's premier exchange infrastructure) and the Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL). Payments go straight from your bank account to the respective Mutual Fund AMC (such as HDFC, SBI, ICICI Prudential, etc.).

How do I start an SIP? What is the minimum investment amount? payments

You can start an SIP in just a few minutes after completing your DigiLocker KYC. You can begin investing with as little as ₹500 per month using UPI AutoPay, Net Banking, or e-NACH auto-debit mandates linked to your verified bank account.

Can I speak with a human advisor for personalized portfolio guidance? forum

Absolutely. Unlike algorithmic-only discount platforms, every client at Vridhi Wealth is paired with an experienced mutual fund distributor. You can schedule a video call, speak on the phone, or chat via WhatsApp for annual portfolio reviews and goal realignment.
