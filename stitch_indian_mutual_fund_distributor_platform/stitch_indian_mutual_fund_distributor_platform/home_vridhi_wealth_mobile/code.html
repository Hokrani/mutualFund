Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AMFI Registered MFD? expand_more

AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) registers distributors who possess verified credentials and NISM certifications. Vridhi Wealth holds ARN-184920, empowering us to assist investors with disciplined portfolio structuring and order routing across India's mutual fund houses.

Are my investments safe with Vridhi Wealth? expand_more

Yes, 100%. Vridhi Wealth never holds your money. Every transaction is directly routed through SEBI-regulated exchange platforms (BSE StAR MF / NSE NMF II) directly to the respective Asset Management Company (AMC) and registered custodian banks.

Can I stop or pause my SIP anytime? expand_more

Absolutely. Open-ended mutual funds provide full liquidity. You can pause, modify, or stop your SIP mandate at zero penalty directly from your investor console or by speaking with your assigned advisor.

What documents are needed to start? expand_more

Only your PAN card, Aadhaar number linked with an active mobile phone for OTP verification, and bank account details for mandate creation. The entire process takes under 3 minutes online.
